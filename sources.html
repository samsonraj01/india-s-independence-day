Sources & Reference Bibliography

Authoritative portals, archives, and academic literature compiled to construct this educational portal.

Official Source

National Portal of India (india.gov.in)

Used to verify historical files, constitutional development timelines, and the profiles of national symbols (national anthem, emblem, and tricolour details).

Visit Portal
Official Source

Ministry of Defence / Rashtraparv Portal

Authoritative source confirming 15 August 2026 as India's 80th Independence Day (based on the first celebration in 1947 being Independence Day #1).

Visit Ministry Site
Historical Archive

National Archives of India (NAI)

Primary repository for original historical documents, colonial-era records, correspondence relating to freedom struggle figures, and local declarations.

Visit Archives
Official Source

MyGov India - Amrit Mahotsav Chronicles

Provided comprehensive historical summaries of lesser-known regional leaders, tribal uprisings (Rampa Rebellion), and women active in the freedom movement.

Visit Amrit Mahotsav Archives
Official Source

President of India Publications (presidentofindia.nic.in)

Speech logs, historical addresses, and profile databases of leaders and builders of the Republic of India.

Visit Presidential Site
